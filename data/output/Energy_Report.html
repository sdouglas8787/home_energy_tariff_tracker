Method

Usage basis: recency-weighted seasonal, bias-corrected (walk-forward). The forecast is a recency-weighted seasonal model (recent months weighted more), bias-corrected by a walk-forward test — at each past month the model is rebuilt on only-earlier data, predicted, and compared to the actual, and the average error is used to calibrate the forecast. The 95% band reflects that test's error spread. Gas volumes are converted to kWh; a supplier's calorific value varies, so figures are a close estimate, not a bill.
